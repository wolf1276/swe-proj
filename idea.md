# Product idea

## Problem

Students need affordable textbooks, lab equipment, stationery and hostel essentials while other students have reusable items to sell or donate. CampusTrade gives one college a structured discovery and contact space restricted to verified institutional accounts. Source: S1 p. 1; S2 pp. 2-3; S3 p. 2.

## Users and value

A verified student can discover an item, offer an item for sale or donation, contact an owner and report misleading content through the same account. An authorized administrator manages users and reviews reported listings. Verification establishes mailbox control and campus eligibility, not item quality or guaranteed safety.

## Main journey

Eligible email -> successful verification -> sign in -> browse/search/filter -> listing detail -> contact owner -> students independently agree on payment and exchange. Owners maintain descriptions, prices and availability. Reports lead to administrator review and a recorded decision.

## Included

Campus email access, student/admin permissions, listing creation/edit/deletion, categories, price/condition/availability filters, contact initiation and records, reports, user management and moderation.

## Excluded

Payment collection, checkout, escrow, financial settlement, delivery/logistics, supervised handover and dispute settlement. Also defer optional feature expansion such as ratings, auctions, recommendations, realtime chat, multi-college tenancy, social feeds and native apps until core acceptance is complete and separately authorized. Photos are optional, not a source acceptance requirement.

## Success

Students can complete the principal flows with persistent data and correct permissions. Search meets the five-second target under a defined normal workload, capacity is demonstrated with at least 1,000 registered users, and availability is measured over an agreed window. No business or engagement metric substitutes for these requirements.
